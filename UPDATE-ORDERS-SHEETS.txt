RAAS UPDATE: COLOUR / SIZE / QUANTITY CHECKS + FULL PRICES IN ORDERS AND GOOGLE SHEETS

Do the steps in this order. Each step is safe on its own: the new website still works with the old
Apps Script and the old database, and the new Apps Script still accepts orders from the old website.

1. DATABASE (Supabase > SQL Editor > New query): paste and run  supabase/migration-order-prices.sql
   (once; safe to repeat; run it after migration-sizes.sql). Nothing is deleted.
   - every new order line stores: original price per piece, discounted price per piece, total before coupon,
     the coupon discount for that line, and the final price after the coupon (all worked out by the server)
   - quantity must be a whole number from 1 to 10 (0 or negative is refused)

2. GOOGLE SHEETS (open your RAAS sheet > Extensions > Apps Script)
   a. Open Code.gs, select everything, and paste the contents of  google-sheets/Code.gs  over it. Save.
      (If your old Code.gs did anything extra, e.g. sent you an email, copy that part across first.)
      SECRET at the top must stay "raas25.netlify.app" (same as SHEET_SECRET in index.html).
   b. In the function list at the top choose  setupOrderColumns  > Run. Allow the permissions it asks for.
      This adds the new columns to your existing Orders tab (L to T), renames two headings, makes Profit take
      the coupon off, and updates the Dashboard and Guide tabs. Existing rows are kept.
      (It also runs by itself on the first order if you skip this.)
   c. Deploy > Manage deployments > pencil (Edit) > Version: "New version" > Deploy.
      Edit the EXISTING deployment. Do not create a new one: the URL must stay the same.
   d. Optional test: run  testOrderWithoutCoupon  and  testOrderWithCoupon , check the two TEST rows, delete them.

   Orders tab after the update (one row per product in the order):
     A Order Date  B Tracking ID (you type)  C Client Name  D Address  E Phone Number  F Product Name
     G Product Wholesale Price (copied from the Products tab)  H Discounted Product Price (per unit)
     I Quantity  J Total Before Coupon (formula)  K Profit (formula, coupon taken off)
     L Order ID  M Product Size  N Selected Product Color  O Original Product Price (per unit)
     P Coupon Code (empty = no coupon)  Q Coupon Discount Amount (0 = no coupon)  R Final Price After Coupon
     S Delivery Charge  T Final Order Amount   (S and T are for the whole order: first row of the order only)
   Columns are found by their heading, so you may move them; don't rename the headings.

3. WEBSITE: replace these files in your repo (same folders):
     index.html                          storefront
     admin/index.html                    admin
     shared/config.js                    new text "Please select your colour."
     supabase/schema.sql                 fresh installs only (already includes step 1)
     supabase/migration-order-prices.sql NEW
     google-sheets/Code.gs               NEW (copy of the Apps Script, for your records)
   Do NOT touch: shared/supabase-config.js, shared/raas-extras.js.
   Then hard refresh (Ctrl+F5) the website and the admin.

WHAT CHANGED
 Customer
 - Product with 2+ colours: no colour is pre-selected; Add to cart / Buy now shows "Please select your colour."
   until one is tapped. A product with one colour has it selected automatically.
 - Size works as before (only the sizes you ticked; required when the product has sizes).
 - Quantity [-] 1 [+]: 1 to 10, minus is disabled at 1. "Total: PKR ..." under it updates with the quantity.
 - Cart lines show the line total and "2 × PKR 4,000". Prices in the cart are refreshed from the current product prices.
 - Size, colour and quantity stay saved in the cart through checkout (as before).
 Admin
 - Add / Edit product: "Original price" and "Discounted / sale price" (per piece), Available Sizes, Colours and stock.
 - Orders > open an order: Original, Discounted, Total before coupon, Coupon discount, Final, per product.
   Orders placed before this update show "—" for prices they did not store.
 - Export CSV: one line per product with size, colour, quantity and every price in its own column.
